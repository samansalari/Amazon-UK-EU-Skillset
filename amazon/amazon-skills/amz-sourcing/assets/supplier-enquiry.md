# Supplier enquiry template

Send identical wording to every supplier so the replies can be compared. Fill the brackets; do not
reveal your target price or your sales expectations. The user sends it — never send on their behalf
without explicit approval of the final text.

---

Subject: RFQ — [product name], [key spec], [first-order quantity] pcs, shipping to UK

Hello [name],

We are a UK-based company sourcing [product] for retail sale in the United Kingdom. Please quote
against the specification below.

**Specification**
- Product: [description]
- Dimensions / weight: [ ]
- Materials and grades: [ ]
- Colour(s): [ ]
- Required changes from your standard model: [ ]
- Packaging: [unit box type, print, insert, barcode label position]; master carton [qty/carton]
- Intended user: [adults / children aged X+]

**Please provide**
1. Company legal name as on your business licence, and whether you manufacture this product
   yourselves or trade it. Factory address.
2. Unit price in USD at [qty A] / [qty B] / [qty C] pieces, stating the Incoterm and port
   (please quote both EXW and FOB [port]).
3. What the unit price includes and excludes (customisation, packaging, labelling, export fees).
4. MOQ for the customised version and for your standard version.
5. One-off costs: tooling/mould, printing plates, set-up. Who owns the tooling?
6. Sample: cost, lead time, and courier cost to the UK. Is the sample cost credited on order?
7. Production lead time after sample approval and deposit.
8. Payment terms and methods offered.
9. Packaged unit dimensions and weight; carton dimensions, gross weight and units per carton.
10. Test reports and certificates you hold for this exact product for the UK/EU market: please
    send the full report with report number and issuing laboratory.
11. Your warranty and remedy for defective goods, and your acceptance of a third-party
    pre-shipment inspection arranged by us.
12. Main export markets and whether you currently supply UK or EU customers for this product.

Thank you,
[name, company, country]

---

## After the reply: follow-up checks
- Does the company name match the licence, the quotation header and the bank beneficiary?
- Did they answer every numbered point? Unanswered points go to the comparison sheet as `UNKNOWN`.
- Are the test reports for this model, this supplier, a relevant standard, and recent?
